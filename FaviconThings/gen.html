💻 Generalization 💻



On this page, you will discover the significance of favicons and selection tools in web design.

A favicon is a little icon that shows up on the browser tab and gives a website a more recognizable and professional appearance by making it easier for users to remember and identify it. On the other hand, selection tools are particularly helpful to developers since they make it simpler to highlight, move, or change particular areas of a webpage, which increases accuracy and saves time.

When combined, favicons and selection tools not only increase a website's usability and appeal to visitors, but they also help designers edit their work more quickly, easily, and efficiently.
